## What can and cannot be measured

The only explicitly owner-confirmed reference is Beltone IR. V1 agrees with its scope and subcategory **1 of 1**; there is no genuine confirmed negative set and no confirmed historical example for most categories. **General false-inclusion rate, false-exclusion rate and classification accuracy cannot be estimated.** An accuracy value of 1 in the machine output has denominator one; it is not evidence of a reliable classifier.

Synthetic controls cover all 23 subcategories and ordinary accounting/sales/transformation exclusions. Additional controls cover narrow legal inclusion, investor-versus-lawyer distinction, retail loan sales with finance words, administrative loan files, unknown/negated Africa mandates, non-African-only mandates, expiry, unresolved specialist risk, substantive FP&A, ESG support and no-approval/readiness gates. These are policy regression tests, not recovered vacancies or historical/model accuracy.

Fifteen actual-source cases were provisionally reviewed as **analyst challenge expectations**, never owner-confirmed gold. Agreement is 13/15 on eligibility; 14/15 on category/unresolved outcome; and 8/10 on explicitly expected primary functions. These figures diagnose disagreements in a deliberately selected challenge set; they are not population error rates. The 273 overall output differences from the immutable older baseline are also not confirmed errors.

## Errors caught while building the initial evaluator

Regression checks caught and corrected incidental loan/treasury/product words admitting ordinary retail sales, foreign location alone excluding potentially Africa-focused roles, “normalisation” accidentally matching Mali, negated African mandate text being treated as evidence, investor legal work triggering Investment Legal, “help” matching LP/secondaries, Treasury collaboration overriding strategic finance and finance-partnering language being mistaken for finance-team leadership. These are development diagnostic examples, not historical editorial decisions. Two incidental-ESG primary-function mistakes and the two scope false-inclusion candidates above remain deliberately visible.

## Recommended next improvements

1. Have an editor confirm a balanced genuine sample, including rejected-for-scope cases, substantive FP&A, direct equity versus lending, ESG/fund support, general versus investment legal and Africa-mandate ambiguities. Keep analyst expectations separate until confirmed.
2. Replace first-match keyword precedence with primary-mandate and responsibility weighting. In particular, finance buzzwords in logistics sales or strategy enablement must not establish specialist-finance ownership; occasional ESG monitoring must not override Investment.
3. Evaluate eligibility, primary category, Job Function and publication readiness separately, with confusion/error analysis by confirmed cohort and explicit abstention counts. Do not optimise for more automatic inclusions.
4. Recover missing responsibility/mandate evidence for priority cases. Preserve availability exclusions and earlier decisions, then recheck source/application status only in a separately authorised monitoring workflow.
5. Keep the seven-area/23-subcategory structure unchanged. Relevant roles with no precise home remain eligible/Needs Review with unresolved classification, including sovereign-risk leadership.
