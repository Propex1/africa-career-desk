# Initial ACD 2.0 eligibility framework v1

Frozen initial evaluation policy: **eligibility/1.0.0**, 28 November 2026. Registry/taxonomy remains the approved seven career areas and 23 subcategories, version 2026-11-28. This file freezes the evaluation rules, not every resulting AI/rule suggestion as an approved editorial label. Further policy revisions require an explicit version and review.

## Three independent decisions

| Decision | Question | Outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| Eligibility | Do actual responsibilities establish a high-quality, relevant specialist finance career with an African location or mandate? | In scope; Needs Review; Outside scope |
| Classification | Which primary activity, subcategory and Job Function accurately describe it? | Proposed valid mapping; explicitly unresolved with reason |
| Publication readiness | Could this specific vacancy be considered for a new publication/republication now? | Not ready; requires fresh verification and approval |

A missing taxonomy home is not a reason to exclude a relevant specialist role. Expiry, an unavailable application page, an earlier rejection or later removal does not establish that the finance profession is irrelevant. Conversely, a valid category, a prestigious employer, a historical acceptance or a model suggestion does not authorise publication.

## Eligibility checks

Assess **financial substance**, **professional finance relevance**, **Africa relevance** and **evidence quality**. Use substantive responsibilities rather than title alone. Evidence is the retained job summary, role context and responsibilities, with original source identity/revision. Requirements may corroborate expertise but do not invent missing duties.

Include substantive PE/VC, private credit, infrastructure/real assets, fund investments/secondaries, DFI investment/lending/advisory, investment banking and transaction services, capital markets, investment research/advisory/portfolio management, institutional markets/structuring, specialist banking/credit, treasury/ALM, corporate M&A, strategic finance, substantive FP&A/business partnering and finance leadership. Relevant specialist risk and investment-support functions can qualify without executing investments themselves.

Assess strategy, business development, consulting, ESG/impact, project and performance roles selectively. They need substantive financial analysis, financial decisions, specialist financing/investment work or directly relevant investment support. Commercial growth, revenue targets, sector knowledge or an incidental finance word are insufficient on their own. Generic retail/product/commercial sales, routine accounting or administration, HR, marketing, general operations and operational transformation normally remain outside scope.

African location or explicit African investment/client responsibility supports relevance. Employer name alone does not. London/Paris or another overseas location does not disprove an African mandate; insufficient mandate evidence requires review. Broad regions and unknown locations require clarification where Africa relevance is not explicit. Reserve geographic exclusion for affirmative non-African-only evidence. Token boundaries and negation must prevent “normalisation” or “no recorded African mandate” being misread as Africa evidence.

## Narrow Investment Legal rule

Potentially include **primary legal work directly connected to investment transactions, fund formation/structuring, M&A or financing**. Require actual legal responsibilities, such as investment transaction agreements, fund agreements/side letters, financing documents or fund structuring. General corporate, employment, litigation, routine contractual or generic compliance work does not qualify merely because the employer invests.

**All initial Investment Legal candidates require editorial review.** Direct evidence may support a proposed Investment Legal function and underlying investment field, while eligibility remains Needs Review. Participation in legal due diligence by an investment manager/transactor does not make that professional an Investment Legal candidate. Fund formation also does not automatically mean fund-of-funds investing.

## Classification principles remain unchanged

Use the existing registry; do not add subcategories. Preserve direct PE versus LP/fund commitments, direct infrastructure investing versus bank project lending, M&A advisory versus transaction services, institutional trading versus structuring, corporate IR versus fund fundraising, and primary activity versus Job Function. Climate, impact, blended finance and cross-cutting asset classes remain supported tags. ESG, legal and fund support functions retain the underlying investment field where evidenced.

## Publication readiness and historical decisions

Check source/apply destination, recorded availability/deadline/lifecycle, evidence sufficiency, classification uncertainty and required human review. This offline evaluation performs **no fresh source verification** and grants **no publication approval**. Its checklist concerns any proposed new publication/republication; it does not retroactively change the status or approval of already published jobs. Historical availability exclusions require rechecking rather than automatic reopening.

Keep original publication, rejection, expiry and removal facts and their provenance separate from new eligibility proposals. Reassessing an old rejection creates a review aid, not a replacement decision. Distinguish:

- Original facts/decisions with source attribution.
- Explicit owner-confirmed eligibility/taxonomy labels.
- New deterministic/AI-assisted suggestions.
- Provisional analyst challenge expectations.
- Synthetic policy controls.

The recovered set has only one explicitly owner-confirmed taxonomy label. Do not convert old publication facts, raw rejection notes, inherited AI proposals or newly authored challenge expectations into confirmed training gold. Evaluate sparse confirmed metrics, provisional disagreements and synthetic controls separately.

## Implementation boundary

`tools/acd/editorial-reference/framework-v1.ts` is an inspectable deterministic offline review aid implementing an initial approximation of these rules; it is **not a trained classifier or production decision engine**. Known approximation errors are explicitly reported in the initial evaluation. All resulting mappings remain proposed, with zero publication actions. The old baseline/history files are retained unchanged for comparison. Source records, shared contracts/types, taxonomy, production, private monitoring and publication workflow are untouched.
