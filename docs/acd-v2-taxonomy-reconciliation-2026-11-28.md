## Classification policy

- Classify primary activity from responsibilities, not title or employer alone. Use one field, one subcategory and one function when evidence supports a fit. Otherwise retain an explicit hold.
- Direct PE/VC/private credit/infrastructure investments remain separate from LP commitments, fund-of-funds and secondaries. Fund support functions retain the underlying investment field and identify their responsibilities separately.
- Development Finance separates investing/lending and portfolio/underwriting support from advisory, project preparation, investment readiness and capital mobilisation. Climate, impact, blended finance and infrastructure are evidence-based searchable tags, not automatic field assignments.
- M&A and corporate capital-raising advisory remains separate from client valuation, financial diligence, transaction services and restructuring advisory. In-house private-credit valuation stays in Private Markets rather than being relabelled transaction advisory.
- Investment advisory includes substantive portfolio advice, family-office investing, asset allocation and institutional manager selection. Discretionary allocation remains Portfolio Management; research remains Investment Research. Generic retail wealth-product selling does not qualify.
- FP&A and finance leadership require substantive analysis, challenge of assumptions, financial decisions, specialist risk/funding responsibilities or leadership. Routine bookkeeping, reporting-only administration, commercial sales and operational strategy/transformation do not automatically qualify.
- Keep Trading, Institutional Sales and Structuring & Financial Engineering separate functions. Additional functions are FP&A & Business Partnering, Finance Leadership, Impact & ESG and Investment Advisory. Corporate IR remains distinct from investment-fund/institutional capital-raising IR.
- Every new or changed AI classification remains proposed. The existing Beltone IR owner approval is the only approved mapping; no vacancy publication is authorised by classification metadata.

## Important responsibility decisions

- **Beltone IR:** preserve owner-approved Corporate Investor Relations, with corporate earnings/shareholder responsibilities; never fund fundraising.
- **Dangote:** Strategic Finance & Investments / Strategic Finance, supported by NPV/IRR capex appraisal, scenarios, funding and post-investment reviews.
- **Orange Business Performance:** FP&A & Finance Leadership / FP&A & Business Partnering. Revenue/margin analysis, challenge of pricing/cost assumptions and corrective recommendations substantiate business partnering. This does not claim investment execution.
- **Standard Invest:** Investment Advisory & Wealth Management / Investment Advisory. Financial-statement and portfolio analysis drive client investment proposals; no assumption of trading or discretionary allocation.
- **Siemens:** retain editorial review. Hedging, cash forecasts and guarantees suggest specialist finance, but accounting/delivery duties and the earlier owner recommendation require confirmation of the primary remit.
- **Strategy Enablement Head:** hold. General organisational execution and operating-model work are insufficient to establish finance leadership solely from references to capital allocation.
- **New substantive finance leadership:** Standard Lesotho Bank CFO receives FP&A & Finance Leadership; MTN finance strategy receives Strategic Finance & Investments. Generic strategy and business-readiness roles remain held.
