## Removal rules

Only a confirmed past published deadline, an explicit official closure, or a disappeared specific requisition corroborated by the current employer vacancy board qualifies. A missing deadline, old publication date, login, CAPTCHA, JavaScript requirement, geography restriction, timeout, or ambiguous error does not qualify. No account was created, application submitted, CV uploaded, or employer contacted.

Normal sign-in gates following an accessible current vacancy/application invitation are not evidence of expiry. A restriction that prevents establishing application status is recorded as Needs manual review. An unexplained 403 is not re-labelled as closure, even if the job is absent from a board.
