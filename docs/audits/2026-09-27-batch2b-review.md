## D. Private-equity / private-markets review

Decision definitions:

- Existing: already one of the five accepted PE/VC/private-credit listings; not a new classification recommendation.
- Yes: a serious candidate would reasonably expect the role within PE/private-credit discovery, subject to separate editorial approval. No listing was added.
- Conditional: plausible specialist search fit, but narrower remit or status must be made explicit; do not add automatically.
- Theme only: legitimate adjacent investment/employer context, insufficient for broad PE-page inclusion. Would be discoverable only through a carefully defined future theme, not a generic employer tag.
- No: advisory, commercial-bank, public-market, wealth, corporate-finance or other work without sufficient private-investment evidence.

Confidence is the strength of evidence for a positive PE/private-markets search fit (HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW), not a claim that a vacancy remains open. There are 5 existing roles, 6 strong additional matches, 4 conditional cases, 26 theme-only adjacencies and 30 exclusions. Theme-only and conditional rows are deliberately conservative; they are not an instruction to increase the PE page from five.

## H. Minimal deadline presentation correction

JobCard previously added a literal Closes prefix to every deadlineDisplay. This caused Closes Apply before, Closes Before and Closes Valid through combinations.

A display-only helper now separates an existing supported prefix from its remaining text while keeping the card's markup and CSS classes unchanged:

| Input | Card wording now |
|---|---|
| 30 Sep 2026 | Closes 30 Sep 2026 |
| Apply before 30 Sep 2026 | Apply before 30 Sep 2026 |
| Before 26 Sep 2026 | Apply before 26 Sep 2026 |
| Apply by 30 Sep 2026 | Apply by 30 Sep 2026 |
| 2 Oct 2026, 23:59 UTC | Closes 2 Oct 2026, 23:59 UTC |
| 24 Sep 2026, 23:59 GMT+1 | Closes 24 Sep 2026, 23:59 GMT+1 |
| Valid through 14 Dec 2026 | Valid through 14 Dec 2026 |

Before is never replaced by by. Dates are not parsed, timezones are not removed, and no times, verified fields, status decisions or source records are invented. The shared card improvement applies to All Jobs as well as category cards. Detail-page evidence remains unchanged. Three regression tests cover absent text, prefix semantics and exact time/timezone preservation.
