## Proposed classification rule

Career area identifies the investment/financing market; a separate function label distinguishes investing, legal, risk, fundraising and portfolio operations. Use employer context only when the job duties demonstrably support that market. This rule and the classifications below require owner approval. Do not infer Private Markets from an employer name alone, or classify corporate FP&A/sales as strategic investing merely to fill a category.

Keep the seven areas and 20 subcategories. Where no existing subcategory fits, retain the job in All career areas/Awaiting classification until an editorial decision. This does not remove any live job.
